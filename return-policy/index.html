Return & Cancellation Policy

Important notice: Raj Enterprises operates as a facilitator connecting buyers, sellers, and financial institutions. We do not directly sell vehicles or provide loans. Therefore, return and cancellation policies vary depending on the type of service and the parties involved.

01

Vehicle Purchase Cancellations

When you express interest in buying a vehicle through our platform, you’re entering into a transaction with the seller, not with Raj Enterprises. Here’s what you need to know:

Before Payment or Agreement

You can cancel your interest in a vehicle at any time before making payment or signing any agreement with the seller. There are no charges for browsing or expressing initial interest.

After Token Payment or Agreement

Once you’ve paid a token amount or signed an agreement with the seller, cancellation terms depend on the agreement between you and the seller. Raj Enterprises cannot force sellers to accept cancellations or issue refunds. We recommend discussing cancellation terms with the seller before making any commitments.

Cooling-Off Period

While we encourage sellers to offer a reasonable inspection period, there is no mandatory cooling-off period for used vehicle sales in India. We recommend thoroughly inspecting any vehicle and verifying all documentation before finalizing your purchase.

02

Seller Listing Cancellations

Free Listings

You can remove or modify your vehicle listing at any time before accepting an offer from a buyer. Simply contact our support team or update your listing through your account dashboard.

Premium/Featured Listings

If you’ve paid for a premium or featured listing placement, you can request cancellation within 24 hours of payment for a full refund. After 24 hours, the promotional period begins, and refunds are not available. However, you can still remove your listing from the platform at any time.

After Accepting an Offer

Once you’ve accepted an offer and the buyer has paid a token amount or you’ve signed an agreement, backing out may result in legal consequences or loss of your token payment. We encourage sellers to honor their commitments to maintain trust on our platform.

03

Loan Application Cancellations

Before Loan Approval

You can withdraw your loan application at any time before it’s approved by the lending institution. Simply contact our support team, and we’ll notify the relevant parties. There are no charges for submitting or withdrawing loan applications through our platform.

After Loan Approval

Once a loan is approved, you typically have a period (usually 7-15 days depending on the lender) to accept or reject the offer without penalty. If you accept the loan and funds are disbursed, cancellation terms are governed by your loan agreement with the financial institution. Please review your loan documents carefully.

Processing Fees

Some lenders charge non-refundable processing fees. These fees are charged by the financial institution, not by Raj Enterprises. Once paid to the lender, these fees are typically not refundable even if you cancel the loan application.

04

Vehicle Valuation Services

Free Online Valuations

Our basic online valuation tool is free to use with no commitment required. You can use it as many times as needed without any charges.

Paid In-Person Inspections

If you’ve booked a paid in-person vehicle inspection and valuation, you can cancel or reschedule up to 12 hours before the appointment for a full refund. Cancellations within 12 hours of the appointment are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. No-shows are not eligible for refunds.

05

Refund Processing

When you’re eligible for a refund for services provided by Raj Enterprises (such as premium listing fees or inspection charges), we process refunds within 5-7 business days of your cancellation request. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Please note:

  • Bank transfers may take an additional 3-5 business days to reflect in your account
  • Credit card refunds may take 1-2 billing cycles to appear on your statement
  • Payment gateway charges (if any) are typically non-refundable
06

Dispute Resolution

If you have a dispute with a seller, buyer, or lending institution connected through our platform, we encourage you to first attempt to resolve it directly with the other party. If direct resolution fails, we can facilitate communication and mediation, but we cannot force any party to accept returns, cancellations, or refunds beyond their contractual obligations.

For disputes involving fraud, misrepresentation, or illegal activities, we take such matters seriously and will cooperate fully with any legal investigation. Please report such issues immediately to our support team.

07

Special Circumstances

In exceptional circumstances, we may consider cancellations or refunds outside of this policy. This includes cases involving:

  • Proven fraud or misrepresentation by any party
  • Stolen vehicles or forged documents
  • Major discrepancies in vehicle condition not disclosed by seller
  • Technical errors on our platform that affected your transaction

Each case is reviewed individually, and decisions are made at our sole discretion.

08

Your Consumer Rights

This policy does not affect your statutory consumer rights under Indian law. If you believe any transaction violates consumer protection laws, you may file a complaint with the appropriate consumer forum or regulatory authority in addition to contacting us.

09

How to Request Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation or refund for services provided by Raj Enterprises:

  1. 1Contact our support team via phone, email, or WhatsApp
  2. 2Provide your transaction ID, booking reference, or order number
  3. 3Clearly state your reason for cancellation
  4. 4Our team will review your request within 24-48 hours
  5. 5If approved, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this return and cancellation policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. We’ll notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our website. Please review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our services after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
