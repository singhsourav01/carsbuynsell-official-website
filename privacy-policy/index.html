Privacy Policy

At Raj Enterprises, we understand that your personal information matters to you, and it matters to us too. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your information when you use our car buying and selling services. We’re committed to being transparent about our practices and giving you control over your data.

01

Information We Collect

Personal Details You Provide

When you reach out to us for buying or selling a vehicle, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and location details. If you’re applying for a car loan through our platform, we’ll need additional financial documents like your PAN card, Aadhaar card, and bank statements to process your application smoothly.

Vehicle Information

For sellers, we collect details about your vehicle including registration number, make, model, year, condition, and photographs. This helps us provide accurate valuations and connect you with genuine buyers.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with our website - pages you visit, time spent, device type, and browser information. This helps us improve your experience and fix any technical issues.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use your information responsibly to serve you better:

  • To connect buyers and sellers and facilitate vehicle transactions
  • To process loan applications with our partner financial institutions
  • To provide vehicle valuations and market insights
  • To send you updates about your transactions, new listings that match your preferences, and important service announcements
  • To improve our services based on user feedback and behavior patterns
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraudulent activities
03

Who We Share Your Information With

We only share your information when necessary and with your consent:

Verified Buyers and Sellers

When you list a vehicle or express interest in buying, we share relevant contact details with the other party to enable direct communication and transaction completion.

Financial Partners

For loan applications, we share your documents with our trusted banking and NBFC partners who evaluate your eligibility and provide financing options.

Service Providers

We work with third-party service providers for SMS notifications, email communications, payment processing, and website hosting. These partners have access only to information necessary for their specific services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect our rights and prevent illegal activities.

04

How We Protect Your Data

Your data security is our top priority. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (SSL), secure servers, regular security audits, and restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis. However, while we strive to protect your data, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we encourage you to take precautions when sharing sensitive information online.

05

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt out of promotional communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us

06

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, analyze site traffic, and personalize your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser, though disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

07

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We’ll notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our website or sending you an email. Please review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
