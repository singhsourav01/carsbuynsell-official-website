FAQ

Questions, answered

What is CarsBuyNSell?

CarsBuyNSell is an automotive trading ecosystem that combines real-time live auctions with instant fixed-price listings, connecting dealers and car owners directly across Maharashtra.

Do you charge commission on deals?

No. There are no hidden commission cuts. You work under a simple, transparent subscription or tier and keep 100% of your deal profits.

How do live auctions work?

Vehicles are listed for real-time bidding by verified dealers, so cars sell quickly at true, market-driven prices under fair bidding protocols.

Who can become a franchise partner?

Aspiring entrepreneurs, existing multi-brand dealers, auto brokers and individuals starting an auto business. All partners complete mandatory KYC and document verification.

What does each franchise tier include and cost?

Basic, Silver and Gold tiers offer increasing levels of marketing, sourcing and business support, with Gold adding turnkey setup and territory rights. Full pricing and details are in our brochure.

Is the subscription renewed every year?

Yes. App access and marketing support tiers are renewed annually.
